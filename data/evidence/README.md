# Priority Evidence 100

Automated evidence-triage catalog for methamphetamine/stimulant treatment, psychosis/dual diagnosis, gambling, family financial burden, and family interventions.

- 100 unique records
- 100 Open Access records
- 97 direct PDF URLs
- 0 records marked retracted by OpenAlex at build time
- 25 review records and 75 articles

**Not yet clinician-approved.** `priority_score` is a transparent discovery score based on design/title signals, citations, topical overlap and OA availability. It is not GRADE, RoB 2, ROBINS-I or a clinical recommendation.

Before use in the production answer context, full texts should be checked for retraction/correction, population fit, study design, risk of bias, conflicts of interest and applicability.
